Compliance

Rights and access were reviewed for every source. This report uses public, authorized, or synthetic material and contains no information known to be confidential to a third party.

Method

  • Prefer issuer-published written transcripts and retain speaker attribution and source locators.
  • Compare guidance only when prior and current target period, scope, basis, unit, and currency are aligned.
  • Score sentiment with the single finance classifier identified in the structured manifest on a −100 to +100 scale.
  • Count hedge phrases in management turns only, preserve raw counts, and normalize per 1,000 included words.
  • Separate company facts, management claims, calculated changes, and analyst interpretation.

Limitations

  • Sentiment and hedge measures are language indicators, not forecasts of fundamentals or security returns.
  • Transcript corrections, incomplete speaker attribution, or provider normalization can change lexical results.
  • Company-defined KPIs may change definition; the report discloses definition, scope, and basis rather than assuming comparability.
  • This fixture uses a fictional issuer and illustrative evidence.
